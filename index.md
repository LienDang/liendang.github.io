## Lab: decode your food

The page has an interactive demo. It runs in the browser, so it isn't available in this Markdown version.

- Type a food, in English or Vietnamese, and the Lab searches [UniProt](https://www.uniprot.org), the public protein database, live for that food's reviewed proteins, food-relevant ones first (storage proteins, milk or muscle proteins, allergens).
- Pick a protein to watch a cell build its first 21 amino acids: the gene is transcribed to mRNA and translated codon by codon. Only the protein matches the real one; the DNA uses the Lab's own codons.
- Each protein shows UniProt's function summary, its signal peptide, and a link to the full entry.
- Click any DNA base to mutate it and see whether the change is silent, missense, nonsense, or breaks the start or stop codon.
- "Shuffle codons" rewrites the DNA with synonymous codons: same protein, different DNA.
- A codon wheel of the standard genetic code, GC content, amino acid classes, and the same logic in Python.
