About

Hi, I'm Lien.

I'm a full-stack developer with a Python-first mindset. I love building beautiful digital experiences and using technology to make life easier for people.

What I care about

Education, care and community impact. I care deeply about building tools that serve real human needs, and I try to work in a way that's thoughtful, service-driven and creative.

What draws me in

Python, data and biotech: the places where design, problem-solving and purpose come together. That curiosity is why this site has a Lab. Name a food, and it looks up the proteins inside it live in a public protein database, then shows how a cell builds one, from DNA to mRNA to protein.

What I build

  • Web apps: responsive sites that blend clean design with thoughtful storytelling and clear messaging.
  • Data: dashboards that turn complex information into useful, easy-to-understand decisions.
  • Product: simple digital experiences built for service, learning and accessibility.

What I work with

Python, JavaScript, React, Node.js, HTML, CSS and SQL; Flask and FastAPI on the backend; Pandas and data science for analysis; and UI design, responsive design and accessibility on the front end.
